The Great Donut Heist
Scene 1 — The Suspicious Package

Setting: A quiet street at night. One lamppost, one bench, one trash can.

A gangster named Tony Tinyshoes waits beneath the lamppost. A mysterious suitcase slides toward him from behind the trash can.

Tony looks around, opens it dramatically… and finds a single enormous pink donut.

Tony: “At last. The most valuable object in the city.”

A cop, Officer Murphy, enters while drinking coffee.

Murphy: “Put the donut down.”

Tony: “Never!”

Tony grabs the donut and runs. Murphy drops his coffee and begins the chase.

Scene 2 — The World’s Slowest Chase

Setting: The same street, with a parked car and a fire hydrant.

Tony and Murphy run in circles around the parked car. After three laps, both are exhausted.

Tony hides behind the thin lamppost. His entire body is still visible.

Murphy pretends not to see him.

Murphy: “Where could he possibly be?”

Tony quietly rotates around the lamppost as Murphy walks around it.

They continue rotating for several seconds, always staying on opposite sides.

Then they both become dizzy and fall over.

Scene 3 — The Donut Standoff

Setting: A small rooftop. One ventilation pipe and a clothesline.

Tony stands at the edge of the rooftop, holding the donut above his head.

Tony: “One more step and the donut gets it!”

Murphy: “Tony, be reasonable. That donut has a family.”

Tony takes a bite to prove he is serious.

The donut is so hard that his teeth begin vibrating.

Murphy tries not to laugh.

Suddenly, the giant donut slips from Tony’s hands and rolls across the rooftop. Both men dive after it, miss, and crash into each other.

The donut rolls safely into a laundry basket.

Scene 4 — Partners in Crime

Setting: Back on the street. A police car is waiting.

Murphy handcuffs Tony—but accidentally cuffs Tony’s wrist to his own.

Murphy: “That was intentional.”

A bakery delivery truck drives past. Its back doors are open, revealing hundreds of giant donuts.

Tony and Murphy look at each other.

Tony: “Truce?”

Murphy: “Truce.”

Still handcuffed together, they run after the truck.

The police car remains behind, its siren flashing uselessly.

Final shot: Tony and Murphy disappear into the distance while arguing about which donut flavor is best.
